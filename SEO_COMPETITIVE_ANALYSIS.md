## 1. `/photographer-athens/` — Primary KW: "photographer Athens"

### Secondary Keywords
- Athens photographer, best photographer in Athens, photographer in Athens Greece, top photographer Athens

### Search Intent
**Commercial** — users evaluating photographers for hire, comparing portfolios before contacting.

### Estimated Ranking Difficulty
**High** — this is the most generic, highest-volume photography keyword in Athens. Established competitors have decade-old domains, hundreds of backlinks, active blogs, and strong Google Business Profiles with hundreds of reviews.

### Competitor Types
- Large Athens photography studios (e.g., wedding/event focused) with 10+ years of domain history
- Individual photographers with strong personal brands and active social media
- Directory/thin pages (e.g., Weddy.gr, eventora.gr, photogeni.gr) that aggregate listings with Google Business Profile integrations
- Blog posts from Greek wedding/event publications

### Why This Page Could Win
- Clean, fast Astro site with perfect Lighthouse scores (a technical edge over slow WordPress competitors)
- Unique multi-style offering (portrait, editorial, personal branding — not just weddings)
- Well-structured FAQ can trigger rich snippets
- Schema.org Photographer + LocalBusiness markup

### Gap Analysis
- **Zero backlinks** — new domain with no authority; this page cannot rank for a high-difficulty term without link building
- No blog or content hub to build topical authority around "photography + Athens"
- No Google Business Profile reviews traction yet
- Competitors have hundreds of reviews on Google Maps
- No local citation profile (e.g., Yelp, Xronos, Greek business directories)

### Recommendations
1. **Do not prioritise this page for year 1.** Target it as a long-term anchor. Focus spin-off pages (neighbourhood, service-specific) first to build domain authority.
2. Create 3–5 supporting blog posts (e.g., "How to Choose a Photographer in Athens", "Best Locations for a Photoshoot in Athens") and link to this page internally.
3. Build the Google Business Profile to 20+ reviews before expecting any movement on this KW.

## 8. `/photographer-athens-prices/` — Primary KW: "photographer Athens prices"

### Secondary Keywords
- Photographer cost Athens, photography pricing Athens, Athens photographer rates, how much does a photographer cost Athens

### Search Intent
**Transactional** — users on the verge of booking, comparing prices. Highest conversion intent of any page on this list.

### Estimated Ranking Difficulty
**Medium** — price-related searches tend to have less competition because photographers are often reluctant to publish prices publicly. Most competitors hide pricing behind contact forms, creating an opportunity.

### Competitor Types
- A few photographers who openly publish pricing (rare in the Greek market)
- Wedding-specific pricing pages (not directly competitive with general photography)
- Directory sites that aggregate photographer pricing (often outdated or inaccurate)
- Freelance platforms (e.g., Fiverr, Upwork) that show rates — weak SERP competitors but high domain authority makes them hard to dislodge

### Why This Page Could Win
- **Major gap in the market** — most Greek photographers do not publish pricing. Transparency is a competitive advantage.
- Transactional intent means this page converts at a high rate even with modest traffic
- Can be linked to from every other landing page as a pricing reference, creating a strong internal linking hub

### Gap Analysis
- Publishing prices can scare off high-budget clients who equate "published price" with "cheap" — need to frame tiers carefully
- Price pages often have lower organic click-through rates because users comparison-shop
- Must be kept up to date — stale pricing damages credibility

### Recommendations
1. Publish a **tiered pricing structure** (e.g., "Essential," "Professional," "Premium") rather than a single price. This anchors value and gives upsell room.
2. Add a "What's Included" breakdown for each tier to justify pricing and reduce bounce rate.
3. Internally link to the pricing page from **every landing page's CTA** — this is the strongest conversion workflow on the site.

## General Recommendations

### Domain Authority Building Strategy

**Reality check:** a new domain cannot compete with 10-year-old competitors for high-volume KWs in the first 12 months. Authority building must be methodical.

1. **Quality over quantity for backlinks.** Aim for 10–15 high-quality, relevant backlinks in year 1 rather than 50 low-quality directory links. Targets:
   - Greek photography blogs / publications (e.g., Photographer.gr, Fosphoto.gr)
   - Guest posts or interviews on Athens lifestyle blogs
   - Local business partnerships (wedding planners, branding agencies, hotels)
   - "Best photographer in Athens" roundup posts (outreach to bloggers who write these)
2. **Avoid PBNs or paid links.** Google's manual action risk is not worth it for a portfolio site.
3. **Leverage social platforms as authority signals.** An active Instagram with location-tagged Athens content + a LinkedIn profile optimised for "Athens photographer" act as indirect authority signals, even though they are not direct ranking factors.

### Internal Linking Opportunities

- Every landing page should link to the **pricing page** in its CTA section — this creates a strong hub-and-spoke structure.
- Blog posts should link to relevant landing pages. Example: a post about "Best Instagram Photoshoot Locations in Athens" links to `/acropolis-photoshoot/`, `/plaka-photoshoot/`, and `/photoshoot-athens/`.
- The homepage should link to 2–3 key landing pages in its navigation or footer (e.g., pricing, portfolio, contact) — but **not all 8** to avoid diluting link equity.
- Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text: "Acropolis photoshoot pricing," not "click here."

### Local SEO Signals

1. **Google Business Profile (GBP)** — this is the single highest-impact SEO action for year 1.
   - Verify GBP with the exact business name: "Alexandra Maria Deli — Athens Photographer"
   - Categories: Photographer (primary), Portrait Photographer, Event Photographer
   - Service area: Athens, Greece (not a specific street address if you don't want clients visiting)
   - Posts: publish 1x/week with portfolio images and blog links
   - Reviews: aim for 10+ reviews in the first 3 months. Ask every client.
   - Q&A: seed 3–5 questions and answers on GBP
2. **Local Citations:** consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across:
   - Google Business Profile
   - Bing Places
   - Facebook Business Page
   - Instagram Business
   - LinkedIn
   - Greek directories: Vrisko.gr, Xryses Selides, Emagister (if applicable)
3. **Locale-specific schema:** already using `@type: Photographer` + `address` in JSON-LD — this is correct. Ensure the `addressLocality` is "Athens" and `addressCountry` is "GR".

### Content Marketing Roadmap

**Months 1–3: Foundation**
- Publish all 8 landing pages
- Write 3 cornerstone blog posts:
  - "How to Choose a Photographer in Athens: A Complete Guide"
  - "Best Photoshoot Locations in Athens" (link to Acropolis, Plaka, neighbourhood pages)
  - "Athens Photographer Pricing: What to Expect in 2026" (link to pricing page)
- Submit 3 guest posts to Athens lifestyle blogs (reciprocal backlinks)

**Months 3–6: Authority Building**
- Collect 10+ Google reviews
- Publish 1 blog post every 2 weeks (alternating practical guides and portfolio showcases)
- Outreach to 10 Greek wedding/event bloggers for inclusion in "best photographer" roundups
- Build topical clusters: "Athens portrait photography" cluster, "Athens branding photography" cluster

**Months 6–12: Scaling**
- Target long-tail, low-competition KWs that emerge from blog analytics
- Repurpose top-performing blog posts into video content for YouTube (indirect SEO value via embeds)
- Consider an "Athens Photoshoot Guide" downloadable PDF (lead magnet + backlink bait)
- Seek 1–2 collaborations with Athens hotels or Airbnb superhosts (they link to you, you offer photo credits)

**Months 12–18: Consolidation**
- By now the domain has 12+ months of age, perhaps 20–30 quality backlinks, and growing GBP authority
- Reassess top KWs; if domain authority has grown, increase optimisations on `/photographer-athens/` and `/photoshoot-athens/`
- If the pricing page has converted well, consider expanding with a "Photography Packages" comparison table

### Expected Timeline for First-Page Results

**Realistic expectations for a new domain in the Athens photography market:**

| Timeframe | Achievable Results |
|---|---|
| 3–6 months | Page 3–5 for low-competition KWs: "Plaka photoshoot," specific blog long-tails |
| 6–12 months | Page 2–3 for medium-competition KWs: "personal branding photographer Athens," "editorial photographer Athens," "photographer Athens prices" |
| 12–18 months | Page 1–2 for medium KWs; Page 3–4 for high-competition KWs: "photographer Athens," "photoshoot Athens" |
| 18–24 months | Potential page 1 for medium KWs; page 2–3 for high KWs — conditional on 30+ quality backlinks, 50+ Google reviews, and consistent content publishing |

**Hard truth:** first-page rankings for "photographer Athens" or "photoshoot Athens" within 12 months are extremely unlikely without significant backlink investment or a viral content piece. The strategy should focus on **niche terms and pricing transparency** for early traffic and conversions, building toward broader terms as domain authority grows.
